LinuxRunner Plasma bridge: GPL-2.0-or-later.

lr-plasma-krfb.h preserves the plugin ABI declarations from KDE KRFB v26.08.2:
https://github.com/KDE/krfb/tree/v26.08.2/krfb
framebuffer.h, events.h, framebufferplugin.h and rfb.h.
Original copyright and SPDX notices are retained in that header.
krfbprivate_export.h is represented by a local import-only declaration.
No upstream executable is patched or redistributed by these source files.

The bridge loads the distribution's installed PipeWire framebuffer plugin and
links its libkrfbprivate.so.5 and libvncserver. KDE/KWin/libei versions outside
the qualified configuration require separate compatibility testing.
